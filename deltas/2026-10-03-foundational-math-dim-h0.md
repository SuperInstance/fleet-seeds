# Foundational math lane: dim H⁰ = dim Fix(Hol) — a wrong bound corrected with proof + pins

Date: 2026-10-03T07:10Z · Repo: constraint-theory-math · PR #2 (branch dim-h0-fixed-space-law)

## What happened

Picked up the foundational-math directive against constraint-theory-math's
open-problem map. The sheaf-cohomology section carried this law (README,
PAPER.md Corollary 3, tree-proof Corollary 3):

> For graphs with β₁ cycles, dim H⁰ ≤ 9 + 9·β₁. Each cycle adds at most 9
> degrees of freedom (a full 9-vector of slack).

**Wrong mechanism.** Spanning-tree propagation makes restriction-to-root
injective on ANY connected graph, so cotree constraints can only REMOVE
dimensions — never add. One generic cycle kills all 9. The bound survived
because it dominates 9 — too loose to be false.

## The closed form (proved)

  dim H⁰(Γ, V) = dim Fix(Hol_r) ≤ d     (Hol_r = cycle-holonomy group at root)

Tree theorem = Hol-trivial special case. Equality iff holonomy trivial.
Proof: proofs/PROOF-DIM-H0-FIXED-SPACE.md (restriction is injective; image
is exactly the fixed space; π₁ generated by cotree loops).

## The pins (tests/test_dim_h0_fixed_space.py)

Two independent computations asserted equal: (a) nullity of the full
incidence system s_w − T_e s_u = 0; (b) fixed-space intersection over a
cotree cycle basis (root-frame holonomies). 63/63 PASS: 60 random graphs
(d=3, 0–4 cycles), 20 trees (dim == d exactly), 30 generic triangles
(1 cycle → dim 0), GL(9) spot check. Bug found en route: the random-graph
generator spun forever requesting more cotree edges than a simple graph
has — pinned by the simple-graph bound.
